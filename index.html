Sabiha-r jonno ekta letter 💗

Priyo Sabiha,

Janmodin-er onek onek shubhechha! 🎂 Ajker din ta shudhu tomar-i.

Tumi amar shob chaite priyo student. Tumi shudhu porashonay bhalo na, tumi ekta khub sundor mon-er manush. Kintu ami jani, tumi onek emotional, aar nijer upor bishwash ta ektu kom. Tai ei letter ta likhlam, jeno kono din mon kharap hole, ei letter ta khule porle abar mon bhalo hoye jay.

Mone rekho — tumi jotota vabo, tumi tar cheye onek beshi sokkhom. Tomar moddhe je ability ache, ta ami nijer chokhe dekhechi. Exam-e tumi bhalo koro, kintu ami tomar result-er cheye beshi tomar chesta, tomar ekagrota, aar tomar shobar proti bhalobasha ta dekhi. Ei gulo-i tomake boro manush banabe.

Kokhono nijeke chhoto vabo na. Mon kharap hobe, vul-o hobe, kintu tokhon-i abar uthe dariye bolo: "Ami parbo." Karon tumi parbe, Sabiha. Ami bishwash kori, ekdin tumi Caltech, MIT, Harvard-er moto world-class university-te porbe. Ami shei din-er opekkhay achi, jekhane tumi gorbo kore bolbe, "Ami Sabiha, ami eta korte perechi."

Tomar swopno ke kokhono chhoto hote diyo na. Aj theke roj nijeke ektu ektu kore biswash koro. Ami tomar pashe achi, sob shomoy. 🤍

Abar bolchi: Tumi joggo. Tumi sokkhom. Tumi ekdin boro hobe.

Shubho Janmodin, Sabiha! 🎉🌸
